# BandWagon User Guide

## What BandWagon Does

BandWagon helps consenting families coordinate possible rides. A family can request help, an eligible driver can offer a seat, the parent/guardian decides whether to accept, and only the people involved receive the information needed to complete the ride.

BandWagon is not emergency dispatch, school transportation, a taxi service, or a guarantee that a ride or driver is safe or available.

## Parents and Guardians

### Create your account and household

1. Sign in using your verified email or phone.
2. Create or confirm your household.
3. Add the students or other household members you manage.
4. Review each person's contact visibility and notification choices.
5. Join the correct community using the link or join code supplied by the organization.

### Request a ride

1. Open **Rides**.
2. Choose the event.
3. Choose the passenger.
4. Choose **To Event**, **From Event**, **Round Trip**, or the available direction.
5. Enter the pickup/drop-off address as needed. Use the suggested standardized address when it is correct.
6. Review the privacy preview.
7. Submit the request.

Phone numbers are normalized automatically. You can enter familiar formats such as `(469) 555-1212`; BandWagon stores a standardized international phone number internally.

Exact addresses are private and are only disclosed when required for an authorized confirmed ride.

### Accept an offer

1. Open the ride request.
2. Review the driver/offer information provided by your organization.
3. Accept the offer you want to use.
4. Confirm the actual pickup plan with the driver.

A recommendation or offer is not a guarantee of safety, timing or availability.

### Student controls

Parents/guardians control whether a minor may request rides and whether parent approval is required. Student contact information defaults to the more restrictive visibility setting.

### Privacy choices

Your verified phone or email may remain hidden while still being used for login or notifications. You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal information through the privacy/support process.

## Drivers

### Enable your driver profile

1. Join the organization.
2. Open your driver settings.
3. Turn on your driver profile for that organization.
4. Enter capacity and vehicle information requested by the organization.
5. Complete any organization-specific approval requirements.

BandWagon does not independently certify drivers. Eligibility reflects the requirements and approvals configured by the organization.

### RouteAssist

RouteAssist is optional. If enabled, set your maximum extra minutes and route-deviation percentage. BandWagon may suggest compatible open requests based on your existing ride/event route. Suggestions never automatically accept a ride for you.

### Offer a ride

1. Open **Rides Needing Help** or a RouteAssist recommendation.
2. Review the general pickup area, timing and seats needed.
3. Tap **Offer Ride**.
4. Wait for the parent/guardian to accept.
5. Exact pickup information is disclosed only when authorized for the confirmed ride.

### Active ride status

Use the active ride buttons as the ride progresses: **On My Way**, **I Have Arrived**, **Picked Up**, and **Complete Ride**. If Verified Pickup is required, complete the one-time pickup handshake before marking the passenger picked up.

## Notifications

BandWagon uses Push, email and optional SMS/RCS based on message priority, organization settings and user preferences. Safety-critical communication may override ordinary notification preferences when required by the approved safety workflow.

SMS/RCS supports standard STOP/HELP controls.

## Blocking and Safety

You can block another user/household where the feature is available. Blocking suppresses matching/contact without notifying the blocked party.

For an emergency, call 911 or the appropriate emergency service. BandWagon does not make emergency decisions or dispatch emergency services.

## Account and Data

Users can use the supported privacy process to access, correct, export or delete their information. Some minimum records may be retained for security, consent, legal, fraud-prevention or referential-integrity reasons and are minimized/de-identified where appropriate.

## Getting Help

1. Check the BandWagon status page if the service appears unavailable.
2. Contact your organization admin for membership, driver approval, event or organization-policy questions.
3. Contact BandWagon Support for account, platform, privacy or technical problems.
4. Never send your password, one-time code, full payment-card data or unrelated sensitive documents in a support message.
