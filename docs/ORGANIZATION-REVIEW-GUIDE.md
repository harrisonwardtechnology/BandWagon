# BandWagon Organization Review Guide

This guide distills the working BandWagon proposal/evidence package into reusable, organization-neutral review language. It is not an approval form and does not replace a board, school, insurer, legal, privacy, transportation or other required review process.

## Bottom Line

BandWagon can be positioned as a private, voluntary tool that helps families find one another for rides. The cleanest operating model keeps schools and programs out of the transportation transaction unless they deliberately choose a separately reviewed integration: parents opt in, parents control student participation, adult drivers offer rides, and no school roster, address, student ID or account data is required for the core service.

## What BandWagon Is

- A privacy-focused matching and communication tool for voluntary family carpools.
- A Progressive Web App that works in a browser and may be installed like an app.
- A way to reduce broad posting of names, phone numbers and addresses.
- A parent-controlled household tool with guardian authority over minor participation.
- A free core platform that may be sustained through optional adult contributions, organization support, grants, in-kind services and approved outside sponsors.

## What BandWagon Is Not

- School transportation, a school-bus replacement, taxi service or transportation network company.
- A guarantee that a driver, vehicle, route, schedule or ride is safe or available.
- A school-owned student information system.
- A pay-to-participate or pay-to-priority service.
- A targeted-advertising or participant-data monetization service.
- Emergency dispatch.

## Core Funding Boundary

The core platform is provided without a participation fee to organizations or families. Optional support never changes access, matching priority, safety decisions or core functionality.

Outside sponsors receive no participant data, ride visibility, direct-contact rights, matching priority, safety influence or organization authority. Sponsor recognition is an independently configurable adult-facing acknowledgement feature, not behavioral or targeted advertising.

## Organization-Controlled Optional Features

Organizations may independently enable or disable optional modules such as:

- student access;
- open-request discovery;
- RouteAssist / route-corridor recommendations;
- SMS/RCS;
- calendar integrations;
- mapping enhancements;
- AI assistance;
- contribution prompts;
- sponsor recognition;
- optional profile fields;
- administrative exports.

A disabled feature should stop its member interface, new provider calls and new feature-specific data collection.

## Controls That Are Not Optional

- guardian authority;
- organization/tenant isolation;
- role-based access and least privilege;
- encryption and secret protection;
- exact-location disclosure gates;
- audit logging;
- incident response;
- privacy requests;
- approved retention/deletion boundaries;
- STOP/HELP controls where messaging applies;
- no sale of participant data.

## Data-Minimization Position

The core service does not require a school-provided roster, student ID, grades, attendance, discipline, IEP/504 data, district password or official transportation record. Any future district or school integration is a separate review decision.

## AI Position

AI is not required for core authorization, eligibility, matching or safety decisions. Those boundaries are deterministic. Optional AI may assist with language or administrative work only when the organization has enabled the specific feature and the provider/privacy controls are satisfied.

## Driver Position

BandWagon does not independently certify or endorse drivers. The organization or another approved authority defines any required volunteer, background, driver-license or insurance review. BandWagon records the resulting organization-specific eligibility status.

## Recommended Organization Owners

- Executive/Decision Owner
- Operational Admin
- Privacy Contact
- Safety Escalation Contact
- Funding/Finance Owner
- Platform/Technical Operator

One person may temporarily hold multiple roles, but ownership should be explicit.

## Recommended Review Questions

1. Is participation voluntary and parent initiated?
2. Who may administer members and drivers?
3. What driver requirements apply, and who approves them?
4. Is any school/program review required locally?
5. Which optional modules are enabled at launch?
6. Who receives privacy/deletion requests?
7. Who receives safety escalations?
8. Are contribution/sponsor settings approved and appropriately separated from participant data?
9. What incident and pause authority exists?
10. Have test rides and privacy/safety workflows been completed before broad launch?

## Suggested Evidence to Retain

- the organization’s official decision/minutes/memo/ticket/email/conditions;
- named owners and contacts;
- launch feature configuration;
- driver requirement configuration;
- privacy and retention settings;
- provider/subprocessor configuration applicable to enabled modules;
- test results for tenant isolation, permissions, notifications, pickup verification, export/deletion and incident workflow;
- any conditions imposed by a school, insurer, board or other reviewing body.

## Member-Facing Baseline Answers

**Is participation required?** No. Participation is voluntary and parent initiated.

**Is the platform free?** The core platform is provided without a participation fee. Optional support may fund platform operating costs but does not change access, priority or safety decisions.

**Who can see my address?** Exact addresses stay private until an authorized confirmed ride requires them. Generalized areas may be used earlier for matching.

**Does BandWagon guarantee a driver?** No. Families and drivers make and confirm the actual ride arrangement.

**Does BandWagon sell participant data?** No.

**Can I delete my information?** Users may request access, correction, export or deletion through the supported privacy process, subject to narrowly required retained records.

**What do I do in an emergency?** Contact 911 or the appropriate emergency service first. BandWagon is not emergency dispatch.
