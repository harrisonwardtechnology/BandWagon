# Driver Guide

1. Complete your driver profile and optional About Me description.
2. Set normal seat capacity and optional simple vehicle description.
3. Set your service area. Today an organizer adds it for you in the Ride Engine page as a center point plus a radius. *(Coming soon: drawing your own areas on a map.)*
4. Set availability/direction or pause matching.
5. Open an event to see requests, or choose **I Can Give A Ride** and proactively offer seats.
6. Review the limited pre-match information and offer all or some seats.
7. After acceptance, use the PWA or RCS/SMS actions: **On My Way**, **Picked Up**, or **Dropped Off**. *(Coming soon: **Still Good** and **Can't Make It** buttons.)*
8. The platform records manual status timestamps only. It does not collect a route, speed or live GPS location.

Driver eligibility rules are community participation rules, not certification by BandWagon or the organization.
