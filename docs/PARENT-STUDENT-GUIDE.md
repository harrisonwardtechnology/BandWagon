# Parent & Student Guide

## Join

1. Open your community URL or find the organization from BandWagon.
2. Enter and verify your email. Email verification is required.
3. Optionally verify a mobile number for RCS/SMS, phone login and notifications.
4. Enter the group code/invitation and complete admin approval if required.
5. Create/join your household and add household members.
6. Household managers choose whether minors may sign in/request rides and whether requests require approval.
7. Choose email, RCS/SMS or both for notifications.
8. Review phone/email visibility and **What People Can See**.

## Request a ride

1. Choose a calendar event or **Other**.
2. Select passenger(s).
3. Choose **Ride To**, **Ride Home**, or both. Round trips are two independently matched legs.
4. Confirm pickup/drop-off and the general area shown before matching.
5. Add an optional transportation note; do not enter sensitive medical information.
6. Choose request visibility.
7. Review the Privacy Preview and submit.
8. If parent approval is required, matching waits for approval.
9. Review driver offers and accept/decline.
10. Use the PWA, email or RCS/SMS for reminders, cancellation, pickup/drop-off and arrival confirmation.

Exact addresses and contact details are hidden until needed and allowed. BandWagon does not live-track rides.
